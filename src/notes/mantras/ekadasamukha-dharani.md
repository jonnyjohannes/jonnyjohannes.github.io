# Ekadasamukha Dharani

## Eleven-Faced Avalokitesvara Heart Dharani Sutra

_Namo Ratna Trayaya_
Homage to the Triple Gem (Buddha, Dharma, and Sangha).

_Namo Arya Jnana Sagara Vairochana Byuhara Jara Tathagataya_
Homage to the noble, all-knowing Ocean of Wisdom, Vairochana Tathagata (the cosmic Buddha of luminosity and truth).

_Arahate, Samyaksam Buddhaya_
To the Worthy One, the Perfectly Enlightened One

_Namo Sarwa Tathagate Bhyah, Arhata Bhyah, Samyaksam Buddhe Bhyah_
Homage to all the Tathagatas, Arhats, and Perfect Buddhas.

_Namo Arya Avalokiteśvaraya Bodhisattvaya, Maha Sattvaya, Maha Karunikaya_
Homage to the noble Avalokiteśvara Bodhisattva, the Great Being, the Great Compassionate One.

_Tadyata: Om Dara Dara, Diri Diri, Duru Duru_
Thus: Om; bring forth, bring forth! expand, expand! flow, flow!

_Itte We, Itte Chale Chale, Purachale Purachale_
Go, go; proceed, proceed; fulfill, fulfill!

_Kusume Kusuma Ware, Ili Milli, Chiti Jvalam, Apanaye Shoha_
In the flower, in the flower’s radiance, arising, blazing with wisdom — may it be so!
